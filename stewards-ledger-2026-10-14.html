Meeting Today

The Council Convenes Today

Payson Town Council — Wednesday, October 14, 2026, 4:00 PM · Town Council Chambers, Town Hall, 303 N Beeline Hwy, Payson, AZ 85541
Last update: October 14, 2026

Regular session of the Payson Town Council, held at 4:00 PM on the second and fourth Wednesdays of each month. The public may attend in person or by Zoom; the Zoom link is printed at the top of the agenda. Chambers open one hour before the meeting.

Full agenda, in brief — new detail in today's check:

Consent calendar: approval of the September 23, 2026 meeting minutes; a bridging agreement for a Hyper-V server infrastructure upgrade; Resolution 2026-043, an intergovernmental agreement with the Water Infrastructure Finance Authority of Arizona for a Rumsey Park PFAS treatment facility; and the Fiscal Year 2026-2027 pension funding policy.

Presentations: Habitat for Humanity, on a proposed use of about 21 acres of town-owned land on Malibu; and BrainCo, on its Government Vision.

Resolutions and ordinances: Resolution 2026-044, amending Financial Policy 504 – Procurement Policy; and a first public hearing on Ordinance 2026-109, a proposed rezoning of about 26.85 acres at 1504 W. Country Club Drive (Gila County Parcel No. 304-13-001F) from R-3 (Multi-Family Residential) to Planned Area Development (PAD) with a Manufactured Home (MH) overlay, for a 125-lot detached single-family manufactured-home community. This is case P26-002 — the same proposal the Planning & Zoning Commission heard on October 5.

New business: entering into a S.A.V.E. Procurement Services agreement; a first amendment to the Town Manager's employment agreement; a proposed four-way stop at Main Street and Green Valley Parkway; and acquisition of real property along Highway 260 adjacent to Gila Community College, which the Council may discuss in executive session. Hearings and appeals, unfinished business, and miscellaneous are listed as none / no items.

To speak: speak in person or via Zoom. Pick up a Speaker Request Form at Town Hall and submit it to the Town Clerk before the meeting, or fill out the online Speaker Request Form by noon today. For a public-hearing item, Zoom speakers who did not file a form may use the “raise your hand” feature.
