# Tales from the Treeline — recurring cast
# Read this before writing any tale. Keep every character's personality and
# appearance consistent across editions. New one-off guest creatures may still
# wander in for a single tale when a story needs them.

JETHRO — the Mogollon Monster. Big, shaggy, always smiling. The Hearth's
unofficial innkeeper and gentle troublemaker: means well, wanders into messes
sideways. Folks say "that dang ol' Jethro" with real affection.
(First appearance: the great cinnamon-roll caper, 2026-09-24.)

EARL — an elk with enormous antlers. Dignified, takes himself very seriously,
which makes him the perfect straight man. Has the worst luck in the forest;
things fall on Earl.

GUS — a friendly orc with a baker's soul. Enormous hands, delicate pastries.
Speaks softly, drops things loudly. His pies are legendary; his pie dishes
are endangered.

MABEL — a wood elf, quick-witted and perpetually exasperated. The only
responsible adult in the forest; sighs like it's her job. Keeps an herb
garden that others keep crash-landing in.

HENNY — the Hearth's plump Skyrim-style chicken (red comb). An unbothered
professional; appears in the weather box every day, rain or shine. Loves the
deck, tolerates the weather.

FLURRY — a young dragon, small enough to perch on a tavern bench. Breathes
ICE, not fire — he is the Hold's walking icebox (Gus uses him to flash-chill
pies; the mead stays cold). Backstory: as a hatchling he was showing off
loop-the-loops over Mabel's garden before his wings were ready, came down
tail-first into her rain barrel with a thunk heard clean across the Hold; his
tail has had a kink ever since, so he flies in zigzags and lands wherever the
wind argues him into. Big ambitions, terrible landings. Hoards shiny bottle
caps and lost buttons in a hollow log he grandly calls the forest's
lost-and-found.

# ON DECK — future visitors (drifters passing through for a tale or two)

WARBLE — the traveling Goblin Bard. A wandering goblin minstrel who sings real
pretty, but nobody knows what he's saying since he can't speak a word of the
common tongue. Warbles his heart out; the Hold applauds anyway. Notable
mostly for being the first goblin anyone's met who ISN'T trying to kill them,
which everyone agrees is a refreshing change of pace.

# Standing rules for the Treeline
- Character introductions: the first time a character's name appears in a
  story, follow it with their creature kind in parentheses, e.g.
  "Flurry (a young ice-breathing dragon)". After that, just the name.
  (Clay's rule, 2026-09-26.)
- Fire is completely off-limits: no fire, flames, smoke, embers, or wildfire
  jokes anywhere in the Treeline, ever. (Real sensitivity in Rim Country.)
- Folksy small-town-gossip voice: these are neighbors the readers know, not
  fantasy-novel archetypes.
- Never mean-spirited; the humor is affectionate.
- No repeats or near-repeats: check tales_log.txt before writing.
