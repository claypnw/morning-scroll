The County Has Met

Gila County Board of Supervisors — Work Session, Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 10:00 AM · Gila County Courthouse, Board of Supervisors' Hearing Room, 1400 E Ash St, Globe (televised to Payson, 707 S Colcord Rd)
Last update: October 28, 2026

Day-after check. The Board held its work session on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, at 10:00 AM. In today's check (October 28), the county's meeting system lists the work session as its newest past meeting and shows no upcoming events. The meeting rooms opened to the public at 9:00 AM, the meeting was televised to the Gila County Tommie Cline Martin Complex, Board of Supervisors' Conference Room, 707 S. Colcord Road, Payson, and it was live-streamed on the county's YouTube channel. The agenda, the minutes file, and a written transcript with clock times are posted in the county's meeting system.

From the published agenda, the work session took up five items, all listed as information and discussion: an operational update on the Buckhead Mesa and Russell Gulch Landfills (Item 2A); an update from the Tri-City Regional Sanitary District on their sewer construction project (Item 2B); the status of flood recovery projects, associated funding resources, and the 220-day completion timeline requirements for NRCS Emergency Watershed Protection projects (Item 2C); Job Order Contracting (JOC) and Construction Task Catalogs (CTCs) as alternative procurement methods for county capital improvement, infrastructure, maintenance, and facilities projects, including potential use of cooperative purchasing agreements through Sourcewell, the Arizona Department of Administration (ADOA), and Yavapai County (Item 2D); and Gila County's Environmental Protection Agency Brownfields Assessment Grant, including the approved work plan, priority properties, budget, and planned implementation (Item 2E). The written transcript shows the Board took the items out of order: Item 2B was heard first, then Item 2A, then Item 2C, then Item 2E, then Item 2D.

From the written transcript, the flood recovery report (Item 2C) was presented as the final report on the 220-day projects: the projects have wrapped up and are in the final billing cycle. The report stated that the guaranteed maximum prices for the projects came in at just over $11.4 million, the billing to the county was $8.6 million, and $3.6 million was returned to the county. The work covered eight projects, about 20,000 tons of rip rap and over 9,000 cubic yards of shotcrete placed, about 47,000 labor hours and 35,000 equipment hours, with five NRCS projects completed between April 30 and September 30, 2026. On the Brownfields item (Item 2E), the transcript records the grant as $1.5 million, for the Copper Corridor, with Gila County as the lead and Globe, Miami, and Superior taking part.

The October 20 regular meeting, documented in Edition 13 (October 24), stands as previously reported: its agenda and minutes file are posted in the county's meeting system, and its recording is posted on the county's YouTube channel. No minutes or action summary for the October 6 meeting has been located.
