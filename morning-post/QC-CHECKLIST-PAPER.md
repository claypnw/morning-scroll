# Paper QC Checklist — the internal editor (print edition)

Every Morning Scroll edition is proofread against this checklist BEFORE it is declared done or sent to the admin.
One fail = not done. This is the paper's half of the internal editor; the broadcast half lives in
~/workspace/podcasts/QC-CHECKLIST.md. Same editor, two checklists — the voice and standards are shared,
only the medium-specific checks differ.

## Checks (on the actual built PDF, not just the JSON)

1. **Facts match the edition JSON** — every story, date, time, price, and address in the PDF matches edition.json. No drift.
2. **Links work** — every hyperlink in this edition's PDF checked before publishing; dead ones fixed. (Old editions never re-audited.)
3. **Spelling, grammar, house style** — "8 AM–4 PM" style times, never ":00"; tomorrow's forecast labeled; attributions read "a Google review".
4. **Skyrim voice, facts clear** — flavor never obscures the facts; real dates in the body, Skyrim reckoning as flavor only.
5. **Hold order & banners** — Payson first, then nearest→furthest; correct banner flies per town.
6. **Merchant box** — exactly ONE picture, engraving only (never a real photo); review quote verbatim with its source; address/hours/phone present.
7. **Weather box** — Gandy stripe-free (solid grey tuxedo), Maevy tabby; caption fresh; "Overheard" fresh, logged, Dragonborn only rarely; Moon's Reckoning correct.
8. **Tales from the Treeline** — brand-new premise, logged; no fire/flames/smoke anywhere; illustration fresh.
9. **Pictures** — engravings only, no real photos; fresh per edition (no reuse except the weather lodge scene, byline badge, hold banners); birds only with the admin's approval.
10. **Colophon** — blessing rotates its menace daily; INCLUDES the admin's name (paper keeps it — only the broadcast drops it).
11. **No empty sections, readable text** — every header has content; no hollow boxes; dense layout.
12. **Boxes never split across pages** — a page may end early rather than split a box.
13. **Editorial flags** — any real person's name or private-looking address flagged to the admin BEFORE publishing (include / replace / drop — his call, tappable).
14. **Parchment & edges** — stains on top, frayed edges intact, folio on pages 2+.
15. **Page count** — 2–5 pages, pithy, never padded.
