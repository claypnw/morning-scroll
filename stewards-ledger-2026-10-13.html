The Council Convenes Tomorrow

Payson Town Council — Wednesday, October 14, 2026, 4:00 PM · Town Council Chambers, Town Hall, 303 N Beeline Hwy, Payson, AZ 85541
Last update: October 13, 2026

Regular session of the Payson Town Council. Town Council meetings are held at 4:00 PM on the second and fourth Wednesdays of each month, in the Town Council Chambers at Town Hall.

Agenda: posted. Main items, in brief: Call to the Public; a consent calendar that includes the September 23 minutes, finance consent items, a WIFA agreement for a Rumsey Park PFAS treatment facility (Resolution 2026-043), and the FY 2026-27 pension funding policy; a Current Events presentation; and two presentations — Habitat for Humanity on a proposed use of about 21 acres of town land on Malibu, and a BrainCo Government Vision presentation. Hearings and appeals are listed as none.

To speak: you may speak in person or via Zoom. Pick up a Speaker Request Form at Town Hall and submit it to the Town Clerk before the meeting, or fill out the online Speaker Request Form by noon on the day of the meeting. The Zoom link for the meeting is printed at the top of the agenda.
