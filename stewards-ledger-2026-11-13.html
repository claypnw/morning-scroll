From the published agenda, the work session took up five items, all listed as information and discussion: an operational update on the Buckhead Mesa and Russell Gulch Landfills (Item 2A); an update from the Tri-City Regional Sanitary District on their sewer construction project (Item 2B); the status of flood recovery projects, associated funding resources, and the 220-day completion timeline requirements for NRCS Emergency Watershed Protection projects (Item 2C); Job Order Contracting (JOC) and Construction Task Catalogs (CTCs) as alternative procurement methods for county capital improvement, infrastructure, maintenance, and facilities projects, including potential use of cooperative purchasing agreements through Sourcewell, the Arizona Department of Administration (ADOA), and Yavapai County (Item 2D); and Gila County's Environmental Protection Agency Brownfields Assessment Grant, including the approved work plan, priority properties, budget, and planned implementation (Item 2E). On the Brownfields item (Item 2E), the transcript records the grant as $1.5 million, for the Copper Corridor, with Gila County as the lead and Globe, Miami, and Superior taking part.
