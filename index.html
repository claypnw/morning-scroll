    The Faire's Final Day — and the Week Rolls On

    “HAIL and well met, friends of the Hearth — Sundas rises over the Rim in a wash of gold, and the faire's last day is upon us: the North Inch village throws its gates open one final time, nine of the morning clock to five of the evening, for one last caber toss, one last song on the pipes, and one last stroll through the artisans' row before the tents come down. Frostfall has settled over the Hold proper now, and the week ahead runs rich: a classic car show rolls into Payson, the fort at Camp Verde celebrates its seventieth homecoming, and Sedona fills the red rocks with wings, wheels, and art. Fill thy cup, traveler — the Hold is wide awake.”
