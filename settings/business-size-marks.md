## How to use these marks
- Scroll merchant selection: skip anything marked **Big**; pick the small local shop nearby instead.
- For names Clay marked **Local**, do not exclude them solely for size; still choose real small/local merchants for spotlights and respect separate editorial rules.
- Separate standing rules still apply: no casino merchant feature (Mazatzal Hotel & Casino and Cliff Castle Casino Hotel were marked Local for size only), while fun and uplifting Tonto Apache Tribe stories remain welcome; hospitals/institutions are not merchant spotlights by themselves.
- Gilded Pages: Big businesses belong in practical categories (groceries, pharmacy, hardware, fuel, lodging, medical) with plain facts and no Scroll citation.
- Attractions/institutions that are not merchants (state/national parks, museums, hospitals in news) are unaffected; this list governs merchant features and directory placement.
