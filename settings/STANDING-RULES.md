## The Morning Scroll (paper)

16. **Skyrim voice, real facts** — flavor never obscures the facts. The paper never carries your personal calendar. (standing)
17. **Page one sets the theme** — no single giant "hero" picture; two smaller side-by-side pictures when two big events share the front page. (2026-09-30, corrected 2026-10-06)
18. **Engravings only, never real photos** — all pictures are old-timey engravings, tinted to look printed on the parchment. (2026-09-29)
19. **Skyrim, not generic medieval** — Nordic timber-and-stone, game-accurate armor (horned iron helmets and the like), fur-trimmed Nordic garb. Never shining plate knights, gothic cathedrals, or generic "ye olde" filler. (2026-10-01)
20. **Skyrim-ify modern things** — real-life objects get reimagined in-world for pictures (a car becomes a wagon with a mysterious propulsion system); words may name the real thing lightly. Never depict cars, not even old-timey ones. (2026-10-05)
21. **Birds: a natural few, never a swarm** — a scattered handful at most; sometimes no birds at all. Natural skies, not a rule. (2026-10-01, refined 2026-10-08)
22. **The cats star in the weather box** — Gandy (solid grey tuxedo, NO stripes) and Maevy (grey tabby). Same lodge and deck every day; only the sky, weather, and their antics change — and the antics must be NEW every day, never repeated. (2026-10-04, 2026-10-08)
23. **Fresh pictures every edition** — recurring events get a new illustration each day, true to that day's actual advertised program (I read the real flyers first). Never reuse an event's picture across days. (2026-09-29, 2026-10-04)
24. **The "Overheard" line is fresh daily** — never repeated; the Dragonborn appears in it only rarely (never back-to-back). (2026-10-01)
25. **The Dragonborn near-miss is a rare gag** — "he was here not ten minutes ago!" at most ~weekly in Stories Around the Rim (Fridays). (2026-10-01)
26. **Tales from the Treeline** — a brand-new ~150–220 word whimsical tale every edition with the recurring cast; fire is completely off-limits. (standing)
27. **The colophon blessing rotates its menace** — dragons, wolves, bears, trolls, spiders, skeevers, giants, sabre cats, then back again. Always ends "Clay" in the paper. (2026-09-29)
28. **Merchant of the Hold** — one small, locally-rooted business per edition. National chains, big-box stores, and large corporate operations never get merchant features. One engraving, one real verbatim review quote (never invented), and a "why the Hearth sends you" line. Never repeated. (2026-09-29)
29. **Flag real names/private addresses before publishing** — I show you the item and you decide: include, replace, or drop. Never redacted or included silently on my call. (2026-09-30)
30. **Check the current edition's links before publishing**; old editions are never re-audited. (2026-09-30)
31. **Payson Hold focus** — Star Valley counts as Payson for merchants; Tonto Basin events only get a mention when worth the drive. (2026-09-30, 2026-10-06)
32. **The Holds are NOT in Skyrim** — they lie just south of the map, below the Rift. Close neighbors who trade with Riften, but fly their own banners. (2026-10-02)
33. **"Hold" is singular, "Holds" plural** — checked in every proofread. (2026-09-29)
34. **Time style** — "8 AM–4 PM", "noon–4 PM", never ":00". (2026-09-26)
35. **Skyrim calendar is flavor** — months and days map 1:1 to real ones; the masthead and colophon carry it, but the body uses real dates ("Sat, Oct. 10"). Never the tiresome "lowlanders call it October" explainer. (2026-09-29, revised 2026-10-04)
36. **Volume/issue from the date** — Vol. = YYMM, No. = zero-padded day (Oct. 8, 2026 → Vol. 2610, No. 08). No counter file. (2026-10-03)
37. **Corrections touch only the current edition** — older editions stay as printed. (standing)
38. **The Dude's portrait changes subtly every edition** — never announced; sharp-eyed readers spot it. (2026-09-29)
39. **No saloons** — drinking places are mead halls, inns, or taverns. (2026-09-30)
40. **Valley Dwellers / lowlanders** — warm and fun, never mean; they're honored guests. (2026-09-29, 2026-10-01)
41. **Tonto Apache Tribe** — written about with respect, always; every story fun and uplifting, bringing everyone up. (2026-09-29)
42. **Seasonal touches stay light** — currently: long nights get a *hint* of vampire humor (garlic braids selling out, extra lanterns) — never an accusation, never overbearing. (2026-10-03)
43. **Your Greybeard persona** — woven into the paper only very lightly when natural; never announced, never takes over a story. (2026-09-30)
